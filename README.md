# Protein Design QuickStart: PyMOL, Biotite, & Alphafold

Hi,

This repository documents my completion of Dr. Linna An's quick start guide.

I created this repository as a record of my first steps into computational structural biology and protein design, and as preparation for pursuing research.

## What I Completed

I worked through the lab's introductory PyMOL, Biotite, and Alphafold notebooks, focusing on:

* Protein structure visualization in **PyMOL**
* Programmatic structural analysis with **Biotite**
* Identifying and interpreting protein secondary structure
* Working with three-dimensional molecular representations
* Becoming comfortable navigating protein structures computationally
